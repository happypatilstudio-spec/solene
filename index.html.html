01 —— Brand Concept

Fashion that remembers
how summer feels.

Solène is a speculative contemporary label built around the feeling of unhurried mornings and the specific warmth of afternoon light through linen curtains. It's not nostalgic — it's present-tense. Deeply human. Cinematic without trying to be.

The brand exists for people who dress slowly, who travel light, who know the difference between luxury and presence. Its clothes don't perform. They simply exist in the world — beautifully.

Campaign imagery direction — natural light, analogue feel, never staged

Emotional Positioning
Quiet confidence. Sun on skin. The ease of someone who doesn't need to prove anything — only feel it.
Cultural Mood
Mediterranean slow living meets analog internet aesthetics. Linen, terracotta, open windows, and real laughter.
Audience
Culturally curious women, 22–34. Pinterest-literate. Emotionally rich. Tired of performative luxury.
Visual Philosophy
Everything moves slightly. Hair in wind. Fabric catching light. Nothing posed. Nothing resolved.

Not a brand about fashion — a brand about the feeling fashion can carry.

03 —— Creative Direction

Light, texture,
and unhurried motion.

Typography
Playfair Display italic
For atmosphere and titles. Paired with DM Sans light for all functional text. Warm, editorial, never stiff. Type floats — it doesn't anchor.
Film & Grain
Analog texture overlay
Light 35mm grain on all campaign imagery. Bleach bypass treatment in edit. Nothing over-sharpened. Softness is the brand's signature.
Cinematic Lighting
Mediterranean daylight
South-facing golden morning. Dappled window light. Overexposed whites. Lens flare welcome. The sun does the directing.
Composition
Movement over stillness
Subjects mid-laugh, mid-step, mid-breath. Fabric in motion. Soft focus edges. The camera arrives slightly late to the moment.

06 —— Photography Direction

Shooting toward
alive moments.

Every photograph should feel found, not produced. Light is the co-director. Subjects are never performing emotion — they're simply inside it. The camera catches up to them.

EnvironmentBefore the day has asked anything of anyone
PortraitThe camera arrived late to the moment
TextureYou can almost feel the weight of it
LifestyleThe unhurried half-hour before anything
MovementClothes don't hang — they move with you
Lens Feel
50mm or 85mm. Shallow depth. Warm focus drift toward edges. Slightly breathing — never locked.
Colour Grade
Lifted shadows. Warm highlights. Faded greens. Film-base LUT. Never HDR. Never over-saturated.
Mood Target
The specific warmth of a good afternoon. Alive. Loose. The feeling you want to stay inside.

The final identity emerged through decisions made slowly, in good light.

07 —— Creative Process

How this world
came together.

— 1
Feeling-First Brief
Not a mood board — a sensory brief. What does the brand smell like? What does its apartment look like at 8am? This emotional grounding preceded every visual decision.
— 2
AI-Assisted Reference Synthesis
Generative tools used to rapidly prototype colour relationships, lighting scenarios, and typographic pairings. Weeks of visual searching condensed into focused creative sessions. The tool surfaces territory; the eye makes every call.
— 3
Editorial World-Building
Campaign narratives developed iteratively — testing story ideas, seasonal concepts, and visual metaphors at speed. Generate, reject, refine. The brand voice emerged through this process, not before it.
— 4
System Design by Hand
The final identity — typeface pairing, colour standards, motion language, spatial logic — was crafted entirely through human editorial instinct. The system is only as good as the decisions it encodes.
